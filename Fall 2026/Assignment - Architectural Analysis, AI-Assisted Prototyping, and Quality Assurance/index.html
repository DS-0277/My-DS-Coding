Netflix Playback Engine Architecture

Architecture Flow:

1. Netflix App - User clicks Play.
2. Playback Services - Prepares session & routing.
3. DRM License - Verifies device authorization.
4. Open Connect CDN - Delivers video from nearest server.
5. Video Player - Plays video & adapts to network.
6. Telemetry - Monitors playback health.
