# Tech Interview Prep - Scaling Paradigms and High-Availability Architecture - AD371 (Week 9)

**1. Objective & Industry Context:**

In technical interviews for systems, platform, and cloud architecture roles, candidates are expected to demonstrate how an application survives both explosive traffic surges and random infrastructure failure. Two foundational questions test whether an engineer can build systems that do not wake the on-call team at 3:00 AM:

1. “Can this candidate clearly articulate the physical and operational ceilings of scaling up versus scaling out?”
2. “Can this candidate build a fault-tolerant system from the ground up, eliminating single points of failure across compute, networking, and data tiers?”

In this assignment, you will deliver a **recorded technical presentation (5 to 7 minutes)** with both your slides and your face visible simultaneously. You will address these topics as if presenting an architectural review to your engineering director, stakeholders, or an interview panel.
